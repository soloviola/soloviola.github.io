Two Weeks Without Coding: What Claude Code Changed for My Product Workflow

2026-02-22

I have not written much code in about two weeks.

Most of my time has gone into prompting and deciding which features I actually want to build. At first, I thought this would make me feel less like an engineer. The opposite happened.

I now have more time to test features deeply, think through what should come next, and focus on improving the user experience instead of rushing into implementation details too early.

The interesting part is that engineering has become more fun. I spend more energy on product judgment and less on repetitive execution.

How much faster am I with Claude Code? At least 10x for the work that used to consume most of my time.

I also remember that about 16 months ago, Victor said that in 3 to 5 years, coding could mostly disappear: agents would replace most software engineering execution, people would wear glasses with cameras so agents could understand full work context, and those agents would implement tasks before people even returned to their desks after meetings.

Now that future looks very possible. I want to start building all kinds of tools, skills, and agents to move toward it.

At the same time, we need new interview questions that evaluate AI coding skills, not just traditional LeetCode-style problem solving！
