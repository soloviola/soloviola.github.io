Qualities of Engs I am looking for

2026-01-22

What are the standard of engs in my team? What are the qualities I am looking for in engineers?

Recently I have been hiring multiple engineers for my team: The content side is hiring across Search, Recommendation, Homepage and Feed. The visual art side is also hiring product engineers to help us with all aspects of discovery, generation and model integration. We definitely evaluate technical skills, but in 2026 with AI tools, technical skills are not the dominant factor anymore. Plus, we are still a startup and we have different standards for engineers.

Looking at the engineers who thrive in our team, what are the most valuable qualities they share? Aside from technical skills, here are the key qualities I look for when hiring:

  1. Autonomy: The ability to work independently and make decisions is crucial for engineers to be successful in a startup. Unlike at large companies, most of the time engineers are expected to take ownership and drive the project forward with minimal guidance.
  2. Speed vs Craftsmanship: In a startup, we ship products fast. Often that means we will sacrifice quality to meet deadlines. How to balance speed and quality is a skill for engineers: "Can you improve the quality while we are A/B testing? Can you come back to modularize the code after we ship the product?" This continuous improvement is something I am looking for, which also implies the candidate's ownership.
  3. Communication/Transparency: No long-winded conversations. Time is precious. Escalate fast when you are stuck. Keep pushing for clear vision/deliverables when you are stuck on product definition or design.
  4. Calm and Resilient: Things keep changing in a startup. A product might be deprioritized tomorrow while it is P0 today. A deadline next month might change to next week. Your product launched but it is not moving the needle at all. An upstream dependency might break in the middle of the night and we need to get up and mitigate the impact. A capable engineer should be able to handle the pressure and keep the team morale high to move forward.
  5. Courage: Take risks, try new things, and learn from failures. If there is a big opportunity but it's super risky, can you step up, take it, work around the clock to deliver it and compete with the competitors?
